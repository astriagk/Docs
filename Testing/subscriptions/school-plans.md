# School Subscription Plans

## Overview

A comprehensive subscription plan designed for educational institutions, daycare centers, schools, and similar organizations that need to manage multiple students at scale. This single plan available on a yearly basis includes all features and flexible per-student pricing.

Each plan contains the following information:

- **Plan Name**: Name of the subscription plan
- **Plan Type**: Yearly
- **Pricing Model**: Flat (fixed price per school), Per Kid (price per student), or Base + Per Kid (base fee + per student)
- **Price**: Base/flat yearly price for the school
- **Per-Kid Price**: Price charged per student per year (if applicable)
- **Min Kids**: Minimum number of students required for plan
- **Max Kids**: Maximum number of students allowed on plan (null for unlimited)
- **Features**: List of included features with keys and labels

## School Plan Eligibility & Restrictions

- **School Plans are exclusive to verified educational institutions** (schools, daycares, universities, etc.)
- Schools must verify their institutional status during signup
- Plans cannot be transferred to individual/parent use
- Minimum student requirements (10 kids) must be met during signup
- Schools can choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly billing cycle
- Quarterly and Annual plans receive volume discounts
  Yearly subscription locked for 12-month period
- Renewal is automatic unless cancelled 30 days before expiry
